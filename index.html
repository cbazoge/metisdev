About Metis Dev

Good software doesn't ask for your attention. It just works, right out of the gate.

Metis Dev is built on over 12 years of hands-on iOS engineering experience, including work on apps used by millions of people. I started this studio to bring that same level of craft to every project I take on.

Named after the Greek goddess of wisdom and skill, Metis Dev reflects how I work: careful thought, real craftsmanship, no shortcuts.

The philosophy is simple: build fewer things, but build them well. Every feature, every interaction, every pixel is considered with intention.
